SPVersion - Human Combat Demo

CONTROLS
WASD        Move relative to the camera
Left mouse  Hold and move mouse to orbit the camera
Mouse wheel Zoom camera
Shift       Run
Right mouse Attack
Space       Dodge / burst movement
R           Restart after victory or defeat
Esc         Exit

OBJECTIVE
Defeat the Ranger before your health reaches zero.

HUD
Bottom left bar: player health
Top bar: enemy health
First two action slots: attack and dodge recharge feedback
Green center result band: victory
Red center result band: defeat

RUNTIME
The demo is designed to run from its packaged SPVersion-demo folder. Keep ExternalAssets next to sp_demo.exe.
